Help Center & FAQ

Common questions regarding solar installation, warranties, and system maintenance.

How long does a typical solar panel system installation take?

Residential rooftop installations (3kW to 10kW) are typically completed in 1 to 3 days after engineering permits and site surveys are finalized.

What warranties come with Sun Son Solar setups?

We provide a 25-year linear performance warranty on solar PV panels, a 5-to-10 year warranty on inverters, and 1 year of free preventive maintenance.

Do solar panels still generate electricity during cloudy or rainy days?

Yes! Solar panels convert light energy (photons), not heat. While output is reduced during heavy overcast skies, energy generation continues throughout the day.

How do employees access their internal accounts?

Employee and technician accounts are provisioned exclusively through HR or Administrator clearance. Employees can click "Employee Access" in the top navigation to log in.
